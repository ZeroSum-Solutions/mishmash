Common questions

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes, anytime from account settings. Switching to yearly prorates the difference immediately; switching back to monthly takes effect at the next renewal.

What happens if we outgrow Starter's 5-member limit?

We'll prompt an upgrade to Studio before anything breaks — nothing is throttled or locked mid-cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Every paid tier includes a 14-day trial. No card required to start.

Do you offer nonprofit or education pricing?

Yes — 30% off Studio for verified nonprofits and schools. Reach out from the link in the footer below.

How is Enterprise priced?

By seats, data-residency needs, and support tier. Most teams land between $32–$48 per seat per month.
