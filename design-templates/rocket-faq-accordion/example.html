What is UI Rocket? expand_more

UI Rocket is a learning platform for designers who want to master AI-powered design workflows and ship production-ready websites faster.

Who is this for? expand_more

Designers, founders, and creators who want to level up their AI design skills and build real, shippable products.

Do I need prior design experience? expand_more

No. The curriculum starts from fundamentals and progressively builds toward advanced AI-driven workflows.

How long does it take? expand_more

Most members make meaningful progress within a few weeks of consistent practice.

Is there a community? expand_more

Yes. You get access to a private community of designers and founders building with AI.
