The collection

Four instruments, twelve of each.

No. I — The Orrery

A hand-turned brass orrery tracking eight bodies in true relative ratio.

No. II — The Chronometer

A deck chronometer, gimbal-mounted, accurate to nine seconds a month.

No. III — The Astrolabe

A planispheric astrolabe for taking the altitude of a star by hand.

No. IV — The Meridian Ring

An armillary ring instrument for reading true solar noon at the bench.

The craft

Three hands, one bench, no shortcuts.

01 — Cut

Every ring is spun from a single brass blank

Turned on a manual lathe, then hand-filed true against a reference gauge cut in 1994 and never replaced.

02 — Set

Jewels are seated one at a time, under a loupe

By the same two hands that will later regulate the finished piece — no assembly line, no division of labor.

03 — Sign

Each instrument is numbered, dated, and signed

On the underside of the base, in the order it left the bench. Never twice the same day.
