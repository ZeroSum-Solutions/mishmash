Symptoms

Four kinds of
forgetting

  • Once the context window fills up, it starts "inventing memories"
  • No connection at all between sessions — every time feels like the first
  • Your real preferences are never remembered; you re-prompt every time

The Fix

The answer isn't
a bigger window.

It's this: write your knowledge, preferences, and history into the filesystem.
Have the LLM read that system before every conversation.

× window stuffing

Cram everything into the prompt — expensive, slow, and it eventually overflows.

✓ files + retrieval

Loaded on demand, never overflows, structured and diffable.

Minimal Setup

4 lines of YAML
and you're started.

memory:
  root: ~/.llm-wiki
  format: markdown
  retrieval: hybrid  # embedding + bm25

You now have a second brain that grows over time. It's read from — and written to — every conversation.
