# testing-safety-alert

A white background + red/amber alert colors + striped hazard borders + big red strike-throughs and pills. Inspired by the `.focus` black-on-white blocks, hero quote box, and high-contrast black-on-white tone of `20260412-ai-testing-and-safety/xhs-ai-testing-safety-v2.html` — but pushed to the register of "alert / risk-control / incident report."

**Visual traits:** a 45° red-black diagonal hazard stripe band at the top, a secondary stripe at the bottom, big red strike-through negation headlines, L1/L2/L3 tiered color cards (green/amber/red), a circular indicator light on the alert box, a dark policy-yaml code block with a red left border + highlighted `bad` keyword, red/green checklist boxes, a Q1 incident bar chart.

**Use when:** covering safety / risk control / incident post-mortems / red-team testing / a pre-launch AI review / policy-as-code; you need the audience to instantly feel "this is serious, don't skim."
